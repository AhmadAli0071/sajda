- **`lib/services/quran_audio_service.dart`** (NEW):

### Quran Radio (Live Stations)
- **`lib/services/radio_service.dart`** (NEW):
  - Source: **MP3Quran.net radio API** — `https://mp3quran.net/api/radio/radio_en.json`

## 2) Adhkar Multi-Language — Arabic + English Complete ✅

### Kya kiya

  - `backend/backend/data/adhkar_source_ar.json` (132 categories, Arabic)

- **English overlay (official source)**: Bundled items ko Arabic-text matching se official English translations se merge kiya — AI ke bina 269/300 items cover ho gaye.

- **Urdu / Hindi / Indonesian skip** (user decision): Adhkar mein ye 3 langs empty hain — app gracefully Arabic dikhati hai. (Ayat of the Day mein ye 3 langs official AlQuran Cloud editions se already mojood hain.)
